# Attack family: MQTT publish flood

MQTT publish flood (dos). An IoT device D, acting as a compromised device: D floods the broker with PUBLISH messages over one connection. D is the subject because the broker is infrastructure (Annex C).

ATT&CK technique: T1499.002 — Endpoint Denial of Service: Service Exhaustion Flood.
