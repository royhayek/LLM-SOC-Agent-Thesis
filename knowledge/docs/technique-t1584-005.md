# Technique T1584.005: Compromise Infrastructure: Botnet

Adversaries compromise many third-party systems to form a botnet, which they then coordinate for large-scale tasks such as distributed denial-of-service attacks.

Generic ATT&CK mitigations for this technique: M1056 (Pre-compromise).
