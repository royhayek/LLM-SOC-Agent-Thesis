# Technique T1110: Brute Force

Adversaries gain access to accounts by systematically guessing or cracking passwords, either interactively against a service or offline against captured hashes.

Generic ATT&CK mitigations for this technique: M1036 (Account Use Policies), M1032 (Multi-factor Authentication), M1027 (Password Policies), M1018 (User Account Management).
