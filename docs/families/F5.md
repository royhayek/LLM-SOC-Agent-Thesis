# F5 — Botnet traffic (`botnet`)

Ticket E1-12 (SCRUM-113). Method: ADR-0003. Predicted visibility (`attack_families.yaml`):
mqtt no, flows yes, honeypot no.

## Status: PROVISIONAL — not run, needs a dataset slice

F5 replays a fixed slice of a published botnet capture (CICIoT2023 [8], or Bot-IoT [6]),
retargeted so the bot is the compromised device D and the command-and-control destination is a
fixed non-routable address (192.0.2.1, RFC 5737 TEST-NET-1) that no testbed host answers. The
thesis does not redistribute a derived capture (ADR-0003), so the slice is **not committed**:
`scenarios/botnet_manifest.yaml` names the dataset, file, packet range, SHA-256 and retargeting
map, and the slice is placed at the manifest's git-ignored `local_path`.

**The dataset slice has not been obtained.** The manifest's `sha256` is a placeholder, so:

- the runner refuses to build a command (`BotnetManifestError`), and
- the repeatability slow test (`test_f5_run.py`) skips, and no tolerance is set.

This family is **not done**. To complete it, Roy:

1. obtains the chosen dataset and extracts the fixed slice named in the manifest;
2. places it at `data/external/botnet_slice.pcap` (git-ignored) and sets the real `sha256`;
3. runs F5 twice, confirms flows-only visibility (source D, destination the non-routable C2;
   no mqtt or honeypot traces), and sets the F5 tolerance in `tests/attack_tolerance.yaml` from
   the two runs; then fills in the assertions in `test_f5_run.py`.

## What is implemented

- `BotnetRunner`: reads the manifest, verifies the slice's SHA-256, retargets with `tcprewrite`
  (source to D, destination to the non-routable C2) and replays with `tcpreplay` at a fixed rate
  from the `attacker` container. Command construction is unit-tested with a stand-in slice.
- The manifest schema and the `tcpreplay`/`tcprewrite` tools (in the attacker image, ADR-0003).

## Visibility and repeatability

Unset until the slice is provided (above). Predicted flows-only; to be confirmed by E1-14.
